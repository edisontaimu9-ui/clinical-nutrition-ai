## 4. Conflict, staleness, implausible-value and scope rules

- **CNF-001**: two live records of one element on one date that differ in status, or numeric values further apart than the element's conflict tolerance. Blocking for: `anthro.weight_kg`, `anthro.height_cm`, `hist.pregnancy_lactation_status`; otherwise important.
- **CNF-002**: `intake.npo_status` (equals True) with `intake.oral_intake_percent_usual` (greater_than 0). Criticality important.
- **STL-001**: value older than the element's staleness window, or undated (criticality important).
- **IMP-001**: value outside the plausible range, wrong or missing unit, not an allowed code, or dated after the assessment. The value is treated as unreliable and never used in a calculation.
- **SCP-001** (scope exclusion): refuse analysis when `hist.pregnancy_lactation_status` is one of pregnant, lactating.

_Are there other populations or conditions that should be refused rather than analysed (for example, on a dialysis or tube-feeding pathway)? Should any conflict rule be added or removed?_
